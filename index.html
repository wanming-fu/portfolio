Plastic Surgery*

Narrative game, 2026

Description

Do you think of the takeaway box you tossed after ten minutes? What happens if treated our own bodies the same way we treat plastic? Plastic Surgery* explores the contradiction of a society that depends on a material it treats as disposable.

A New Light

3D puzzle game, 2026

Description

You've just moved in, but something about the house seems a little off...

A New Light is a 3D puzzle game centred around light and reflection, where players push and jump over objects to escape a house that has turned against them. Developed in the Immersive Environments studio, the game combines early prototypes exploring different "forces": Sue-jee's reflections and Wanming's entrapping objects.

Machivium

Unity environment, 2025

Description

Machivium is an imagining of an alternate future branching from the 90s, where scientists discovered how to create intelligent artificial sentience. The creatures they made learned how to adopt the form of objects around them. They evolved to possess a different set of senses from humans, sharing the ability of sight and sound, yet sensing emotion and time in their own abstract and detailed way. With an emotional range similar to humans but vastly different motivations and an ability to shape the world around them with pure thought, they eradicated humanity and every sense of the world we currently know. Now they have learned how to feel for others. Lingering wisps of past emotions call to them, human and non-human.
